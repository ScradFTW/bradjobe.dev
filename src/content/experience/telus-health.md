[RAPIDS Health](https://rapidshealth.com) was founded in 2020 by psychiatrist Dr. Diane McIntosh and is supported by TELUS. The company builds AI-assisted clinical decision support for mental healthcare. I work as a senior developer on the RAPIDS Mental Health rules-based guidance system, and I'm the **lead developer on [Ori](https://rapidshealth.com/ori/)**, the agent orchestration application behind our mental health chatbot. Ori is built for primary care clinicians who don't have a psychiatrist available to consult. They ask a question in plain language and get evidence-based answers on medication and treatment for major depressive disorder, generalized anxiety, ADHD, insomnia, or bipolar disorder: dosing, tolerability, side effects, how to switch or cross-taper someone off a drug, what's safe during pregnancy and breastfeeding.

I lead the cloud infrastructure that Ori and [Soli](https://rapidshealth.com/soli/) rely on. I help design the safety guardrails around the agent workflows and built a **judge-based eval workflow** to harden responses before they reach a clinician. Underneath it all is the platform: **30+ Cloud Run services and functions**, deployed across multiple regions, all defined in Terraform. I optimized our GCP billing and cut overall costs down by **40% year over year**. Day to day I watch production, respond to the automated bug alerts, and coordinate with our team to handle bugs and outages. I also mentor QA and the junior developers, and our automated Claude Code jobs that automatically solve issues in our systems.
